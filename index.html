What the import can and cannot do

Quest prerequisite detection supports clear text such as “Must have completed”, “Requires level”, “Reputation Rank”, and quoted required items. Ambiguous text remains unparsed, alternative quest paths are not combined, and nothing is marked completed automatically. Imported wiki metadata is community-maintained and not an official game API. Some methods and quest ingredients may be incomplete or outdated. An item may have multiple ways to obtain it. Prices in merge shops must be checked on the item and shop pages before crafting. Quest completion and server availability are not derived from these files.
