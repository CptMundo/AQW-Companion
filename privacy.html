Privacy & Optional Analytics

Last updated: 23 November 2026

AQW Companion is an unofficial fan-made utility for AdventureQuest Worlds (AQW), maintained by CptMundo. It is not affiliated with Artix Entertainment.

Your game data stays in your browser

Inventory exports, wishlist entries, goals, Forge unlocks and saved progress are processed and stored locally on your device where browser storage is available. AQW Companion does not transmit your inventory files, account exports or personal backups to a server operated by the project. If you share an export yourself, that choice is yours.

Optional Google Analytics

We ask for explicit permission before loading Google Analytics 4. If you choose Allow usage statistics, Google's analytics tag may send website-usage information to Google, including page views, browser/device information, approximate location derived from network data, and other measurement data controlled by your Google Analytics settings. Google Analytics may use cookies and similar technologies after permission.

The measurement ID is G-JF725WDHV1. AQW Companion does not send inventory content, wishlist details, usernames, uploaded file contents or other game-account data as custom analytics events. We do not enable ad personalization through this integration. Google independently processes analytics data under its own policies; see Google's Privacy Policy.

If you choose No thanks, we do not load the Google Analytics tag. You can change your choice at any time using Privacy & analytics settings on the main page. Disabling analytics prevents future collection from this browser after a page reload; it cannot withdraw information Google already processed. You can also clear browser cookies/storage through your browser settings.

Analytics data retention

Analytics data is managed within the owner's Google Analytics account and subject to its retention settings and Google's terms. The Companion does not give the project owner access to individual visitors' inventory files.

Contact & updates

For questions about this fan project or this privacy notice, contact the project maintainer through the AQW Companion GitHub repository. This notice may change as site features evolve.
